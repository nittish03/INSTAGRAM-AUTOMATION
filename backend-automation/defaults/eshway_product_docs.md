# Eshway — Product Context (for campaigns)

Eshway is a digital solutions company focused on:

- Custom website development and redesigns
- Full-stack web applications and digital products
- E-commerce sites and booking / lead-generation systems
- AI solutions, automation, and technical integrations
- Backend/API development, deployment, and maintenance
- Responsive, mobile-first web experiences

## Who we reach on Instagram

### Website development clients (CLIENT)
Founders, coaches, consultants, local businesses, creators with businesses, service firms, e-commerce brands, and professional practices that need a website, redesign, storefront, landing page, or digital product.

### Agencies and creatives (COLLABORATION)
Branding, graphic design, UI/UX, marketing, social media, SEO, content, creative studios, no-code consultants, and freelancers who own client relationships and need a technical / development partner.

Ideal collaboration model: the partner keeps creative/strategy/client ownership; Eshway handles development, integrations, AI/backend, and maintenance when projects need it.

## Positioning rules (campaign context)

- Match the offer to the prospect: client site need vs recurring agency collaboration.
- Prefer outcomes (polished site, stronger online presence, complete client delivery) over dumping the full service list.
- Do not force a website pitch onto a clear collaboration partner, or a collab pitch onto a clear direct client.
